# Code of Conduct

YayGUI adopts the **[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)** by reference. Please read the full text there; it is the authoritative version.

## Summary

We want this project to be a welcoming place for people of every background and experience level, including newer Arch users. In short:

- Be respectful and assume good intent, especially when someone is new to AUR packaging or PKGBUILD review.
- Keep discussion constructive. Critique code and proposals, not people.
- No harassment, personal attacks, discrimination, or publishing others' private information.
- Keep unrelated or promotional content out of issues and discussions.

Unacceptable behaviour may lead to warnings, temporary bans or permanent removal from project spaces, as described in the Covenant.

## Enforcement

Report concerns privately through GitHub: use [private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/ijaj/yay-gui/security/advisories/new) for this repository, or contact a maintainer directly on GitHub. Reports are handled confidentially, and the reporter's identity is kept private unless they consent otherwise.
